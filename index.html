Welcome to Łąkorz

A two-story forest house where families reconnect with nature

Nestled in the heart of Łąkorz, surrounded by pristine lakes and endless forest, our house is the perfect escape for families looking to slow down and enjoy nature together. Just 200 meters from Lake Osetno, you'll wake up to birdsong and fall asleep to the sound of the wind in the trees.

The ground floor features a fully equipped kitchen, a cozy living room, and a bathroom. Upstairs you'll find two comfortable bedrooms. The house is done in a very classic style — beautiful wooden details throughout, where wood and classic design combine perfectly to create a warm, timeless atmosphere. Outside, enjoy the campfire area, a private sauna, and a refreshing plunge pool.

The region is a paradise for nature lovers — multiple lakes within walking or biking distance, forests full of mushrooms and berries, and charming towns like Toruń and Iława just a short drive away. It's also perfect for hiking, biking, and simply being surrounded by wildlife. Around the house you'll regularly spot squirrels, hedgehogs, rabbits, and deer.
